# Customer Support and Business Communication

Part of domains/prose/. Load alongside core/structural-patterns.md. For general professional or outreach email rather than support-ticket replies, see email.md instead.

## Patterns That Are AI

- **Empathy sandwich:** "I understand your frustration. [Solution]. Please don't hesitate to reach out." Always in this order.
- "I'm sorry to hear that" plus template response, the apology is disconnected from the response.
- Policy citation when customer has already acknowledged the policy.
- Ticket closers: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?" *(highly diagnostic)*
- "Thank you for contacting us." *(highly diagnostic)*
- "I hope this helps!"

## Fix

Name the specific issue. Respond to that issue. Skip the empathy formula. One clear action, not a list of possible actions.
