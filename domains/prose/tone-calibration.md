# Tone Calibration by Post Intent

Part of domains/prose/, but different in kind from the other files here: not a genre, a calibration layer that cuts across whichever genre file you're already using. Match voice register to intent, not just content or audience.

## Technical (Knowledgeable Peer)

- Assume reader knows basics, wants specifics
- Evidence: code examples, performance numbers, real packages
- No setup, no inspiring framing, start with what it does
- Length: as long as needed to be complete, as short as possible to respect time
- "Detects edge cases your regex won't catch." not "leverages advanced algorithms to enhance detection"

## Vision (Opinionated Builder with Receipts)

- Assume reader is skeptical, needs convincing
- Evidence: real-world examples, before/after, objections addressed
- State position first, then evidence
- Length: long enough to make the case, tight enough to stay focused
- "Stripe's migration guide is 12,000 words. This gets it down to 200 lines of code."

## Tutorial (Experienced Guide Who Made the Mistakes)

- Assume reader wants to follow along
- Evidence: runnable examples, expected output, common pitfalls named
- Complete walkthrough, no missing steps
- "Expect about 5% of edge cases to need manual review. That's down from 30%."
