## LinkedIn Formula

Announce → gratitude → life lesson → 🚀 emoji. Appears at diagnostic rates.

- "Thrilled to share..." / "Excited to announce..."
- "Grateful for the support of my incredible team."
- "If there's one thing I've learned, it's that [generic lesson]."
- 🚀🎉 as punctuation for every announcement
- "rockstar" as a team descriptor

## What Reads as Human on LinkedIn

- Specific outcome, specific number, specific context
- An opinion that someone could disagree with
- A mistake that was made and what was learned from it
- No life lesson unless it's genuinely earned by the specific situation described
