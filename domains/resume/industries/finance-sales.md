# Finance / Accounting / Sales

Part of domains/resume/industries/. See README.md in this folder for how this fits together. Load alongside domains/resume/patterns.md.

These three fields share one file because they share the defining tell: a number that looks like evidence and is not. Grouping them keeps that pattern in one place instead of restating it three times.

## Why This File Carries an Extra Warning

Sales and accounting are the two fields where the LLM self-preference effect measured in patterns.md's Machine Screen section was strongest. Candidates in these fields get the largest automated-screening advantage from a machine-written resume and face the sharpest human rejection for the same document. The split that section describes is widest here, so read it before working through this one.

## The Canonical Tell

"Drove revenue growth through strategic initiatives" for sales, and "Managed financial processes and reporting" for accounting and finance. Both name an outcome category rather than an outcome, and both survive being moved to any other candidate's resume in the same field without a single edit, which is the test.

## Percentages Without Denominators

This is the field's signature move and the reason these three sit together. A percentage is only a claim when the base is known, and these fields produce percentages whose bases are routinely omitted.

- **"Grew revenue 40%."** From what to what, over what period, across what territory? Forty percent of a $50,000 book and forty percent of a $50 million book are not the same accomplishment, and the resume that omits the base is usually omitting the smaller one.
- **"Exceeded quota by 120%."** Quota attainment is meaningless without the quota. A number without the target it was measured against, the size of the territory, and the length of the period is a shape, not a result.
- **"Reduced close time by 30%."** From how many days to how many days. A close cycle going from 20 days to 14 is a real accomplishment; the percentage alone does not distinguish it from 3 days to 2.

The fix is the same in each case and costs one clause: name the base, the period, or the target. A candidate who actually did the work has the number. A model asked to sound quantified does not, which is precisely why the percentage arrives naked.

## Named Category Instead of Named System

Same mechanism as the other industry files. *Financial systems*, *reporting tools*, *CRM*, and *accounting software* are categories standing in front of proper nouns: SAP, NetSuite, Hyperion, QuickBooks, Workday Financials, Salesforce. The named standard matters as much as the named tool, since GAAP, IFRS, SOX, and ASC 606 are the terms a reviewer and a parser both search for, and "regulatory requirements" matches none of them.

Naming has a ceiling here the way it does in tech.md. Excel is the baseline expectation in every one of these roles, so listing it is not a differentiator. The specific version names what was built in it: a model, a reconciliation, a forecast with a stated horizon.

## This Field's "Best Practices"

**"Financial controls"** and **"strategic financial leadership"** in finance, **"consultative selling"** and **"relationship building"** in sales. Each sounds like a methodology and names none. The test from tech.md transfers directly: replace the phrase with the specific control, the specific stage of the pipeline, or the specific account, or cut it.

## Fabrication Risk

Extends patterns.md's Fabrication Risk into the claims this field makes easy to inflate, and a model asked to strengthen the resume will reach for all of them:

- **Quota attainment and deal size**, the most inflated numbers in the field and the most routinely verified during reference checks
- **P&L ownership**, where *managed a $40M P&L* quietly replaces *reported on* or *contributed to*
- **Credential status**, where CPA, CFA, and Series 7 are listed as held while a level is outstanding, a license is lapsed, or an exam is scheduled

Any of these can be true. The point is that none of them can be invented on the candidate's behalf, and a rewrite pass is where invention happens.
